# Technology-Enabled Supply Chain Management in the Commercial Vehicles Industry

### A Study of Supply Chain Management at Tata Motors Commercial Vehicles, Lucknow Plant

## 📌 Project Overview

This project explores the role of digital technologies, inventory management techniques, and lean manufacturing practices in improving supply chain efficiency in the commercial vehicle industry.

The study is based on my summer internship in the Supply Chain Management Department at **Tata Motors Limited, Commercial Vehicles Business Unit, Lucknow Plant**, from June 5, 2026, to July 6, 2026.

It examines how technology-enabled processes and continuous improvement methods support material flow, warehouse operations, inventory accuracy, and production continuity.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Understand supply chain operations in automotive manufacturing.
- Explore digital warehouse and material-handling technologies.
- Study inventory control and optimization techniques.
- Examine lean manufacturing and continuous improvement practices.
- Understand the role of data-driven decision-making in SCM.

## 🔍 Key Areas Covered

### 1. Digital Supply Chain Technologies
- **Pick-to-Light (PTL):** Visual picking systems for improved accuracy and warehouse productivity.
- **Hand-Held Terminals (HHT):** Barcode-based data collection, inventory tracking, and real-time updates.
- **Samprawah:** Smart kitting and line replenishment process supporting integrated material flow.

### 2. Inventory Management
- ABC Analysis
- VED Analysis
- Cycle Counting
- Inventory Turnover Analysis
- Economic Order Quantity (EOQ)
- Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

### 3. Lean Manufacturing
- 5S Workplace Organization
- Kaizen and Continuous Improvement
- Kanban and Just-in-Time (JIT)
- Waste Elimination (TIMWOOD)
- Process Optimization
- Work Study and Time Study

## 🛠️ Concepts & Tools

- Supply Chain Management (SCM)
- Production Planning and Control (PPC)
- Material Requirement Planning (MRP)
- Warehouse Management
- Inventory Optimization
- Industry 4.0
- Data-driven Decision-making

## 📈 Key Learnings

- Importance of real-time inventory visibility and material traceability.
- Role of digital technologies in reducing picking errors and material delays.
- Application of inventory classification and performance indicators.
- Integration of lean principles into warehouse and production operations.
- Importance of coordination between suppliers, warehouses, logistics, and production teams.
